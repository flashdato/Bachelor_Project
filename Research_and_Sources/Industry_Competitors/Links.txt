COMPETITIVE SUMMARY
=============================================================================

Key finding: No product below $200 offers all three of:
  (a) TDS / water quality monitoring
  (b) Automated fertigation with feedback control
  (c) Remote WiFi/cloud control
  (d) Open communication protocol

Smart Flow 2.0 is the only system combining all four at ~$70.
