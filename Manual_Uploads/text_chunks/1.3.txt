Smart Flow 2.0 is a functional prototype. It was assembled and tested in a controlled environment, on real farms and gardens, but of course not implemented on huge-sized farmlands. The purpose of the prototype was to prove that all the key systems could be built and made to work together — not to run a long-term agricultural study.

This thesis will cover the complete hardware design of the sensor module, relay control module, and ESP32 gateway. The firmware for both STM32 microcontrollers is finished and tested. The ESP32 gateway architecture has been designed and documented. The Android mobile app was developed with React Native and a Firebase backend. The app integrates a weather API for irrigation recommendations. The fertilizer dosing system is fully implemented in hardware and firmware, a stepper-driven valve controlled by live TDS readings. The thesis also includes a cost comparison of Smart Flow 2.0 with commercial irrigation products. All of these are fundamental steps of a successful automatic and remotely controlled irrigation system.

The data which is lacking is of course time related things, testing measurements validity of sensors and e.t.c these factors are time-related, and for a project of several months with limited funding, I was not able to do more. On the technical side, there are also some bugs related to the system working properly, but the general design and prototype are there and working.
