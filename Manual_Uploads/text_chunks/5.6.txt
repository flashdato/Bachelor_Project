The prototype demonstrated that the core design works. TDS measurement is accurate within acceptable tolerances. UART communication between all modules is reliable. The irrigation sequence state machine ran correctly across all tested cycles. The stepper valve responded correctly to TDS threshold triggers.

Things that worked better than expected:

The division of responsibilities between the two STM32 modules eliminated a class of timing problems. The Sensor Module runs measurement and sequence logic at a stable 1 Hz tick completely independently of anything happening on the relay side. The Relay Module is fast enough at 8 MHz HSI to decode commands and toggle outputs without any observable delay.

The stepper-based fertilizer control, rather than a fixed-time injection approach, proved more appropriate for a system that runs multiple irrigation zones at different durations. The fertilizer concentration tracks the TDS target regardless of how long a given zone runs.

Things that need improvement:

pH calibration must be completed before the sensor provides meaningful data. The hardware and buffer logic are correct; the probe-specific offset is the only missing step.

The ESP32 firmware needs to be implemented before the system can operate as a connected device. The design is complete; the implementation work is estimated and documented in the repository.

No pressure readings are used by the control logic. Pressure monitoring would enable detection of blocked valves, empty supply lines, or pipe failures.
